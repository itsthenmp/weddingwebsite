A Story,

Based in Kolkata, Memories in Pixels is a wedding and event photography brand led by Nitesh Mehera. Since 2019, we have been crafting visual stories rooted in the emotions that make every celebration unique.

With more than 16 years of personal photography experience, Nitesh brings an instinct for light, composition and storytelling. Our dynamic team of photographers and filmmakers works across India, capturing intimate moments and grand celebrations with a refined, cinematic approach.
